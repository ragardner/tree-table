# Contributing to tree-table-core

Thank you for your interest in contributing to tree-table-core! Before contributing, please read the following terms.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

By contributing to tree-table-core, you license your work under Apache-2.0 to ragardner, the sole owner, and acknowledge:

- You retain copyright to your contribution but grant ragardner perpetual rights to use it in tree-table-core.
- Contributing gives no ownership or control over the repository.
- The contribution is yours to share.

Please add "I agree to the tree-table-core CLA" to your contribution (e.g., PR comment).

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Make your changes and test them thoroughly.
3. Submit a pull request with a clear description of your changes.
4. Include the CLA agreement statement in your PR.
